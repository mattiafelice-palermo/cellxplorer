## Scientific contract

For verified canonical BioLogic GCPL sources whose raw directional energy column
is absent, calculate **net directional electrical energy** from recorded primary
cell voltage and measured directional capacity increments. Preserve existing
measured energy and do not fill holes in an existing measured counter.

For each mechanically adjacent pair within one source, global cycle, executed
step, current direction and acquisition segment:

`delta_energy_mWh = (V_previous + V_current) / 2 * delta_capacity_mAh`.

- Use canonical signed `voltage_v`, including verified Ewe-minus-Ece cell voltage.
  Negative-voltage intervals subtract; never rectify samples or energy totals.
- Sum the signed intervals over each full cycle/direction. A negative net total
  is unavailable for the existing nonnegative directional energy quantities.
  This explicit net interpretation supersedes an unsigned-sample interpretation
  for the calculated fallback only; measured counters retain their semantics.
- Restore acquisition order within each source for paired GCPL global cycles.
  Do not bridge record-index gaps, steps, cycle boundaries or acquisition breaks.
  Unresolved positive transfers, counter reversals, missing endpoints, insufficient
  participation or capacity-reconciliation failure withhold that direction.
- Proven zero-transfer intervals contribute zero even if their voltage is absent
  or negative. A single isolated sample does not prove zero delivered energy.
- Reconcile integrated capacity against the existing directional cycle summary
  with explicit tight numerical tolerance. No current/time reconstruction,
  mean-voltage multiplication or inferred unrecorded boundary transfer.
- Preserve independent directions and later per-cycle values. Missing net energy
  stops only dependent cumulative quantities from the first gap, before filters.
  Recompute energy efficiency from the same resolved directional values.
- Native/calculated/mixed/unavailable provenance and reasons accompany per-cycle,
  cumulative, group, inspection and export values. Nominal capacity is unrelated.
- User refinement: keep FEC/energy explanations as short phrases with an info
  button; put formulas, references, sign policy and unavailable reasons in its
  hover/focus tooltip. Keep the figure qualification concise.

## Architecture and verification

Keep primitive Parquet values and parser/calculation identities unchanged. This
is a versioned Cycles response derivation over immutable cached measurements.
Restrict raw reads to eligible verified GCPL sources; preserve the ordinary
Neware fast path. Bound any compact memo by bytes and entry count. No-inode
filesystems bypass memoization and version persistent results by derived energy
outcome, including voltage changes, basis and reason.

Required cases: analytic trapezoids and multi-step sums; native precedence and
native holes; both directions; signed-voltage cancellation and negative net;
zero-transfer intervals; counter/record/acquisition/time gaps; insufficient data;
capacity reconciliation; paired global cycles; fixed groups and hidden cycles;
no-inode voltage-only changes; units/mass/efficiency; saved/export/portable parity.
Re-run the real-source 1–15 investigation in isolation, both private corpora,
canonical local/clean-Windows preflight, frozen checks and independent Astra review.

The investigated source has negative instantaneous discharge voltage from cycle 9,
but positive signed net discharge energy through cycle 11. Net discharge becomes
negative at cycle 12. These are different findings; do not describe cycles 9–11
as wholly positive-voltage data or turn cycles 12 onward into positive energy.
