The review required two non-overlapping timing trees: complete production request and engine/Cell partition, including SQL/header loads, raw reader children, sorting/filtering, post-downsample materialization, Python-object projection, cache persistence and response serialization. The request and engine residuals had to be explicit rather than hidden.

Raw decode is therefore **not** accepted as the dominant exclusive bottleneck.
